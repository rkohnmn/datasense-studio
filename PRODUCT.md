# PRODUCT.md — DataSense Studio

## Register
scientific-instrument-atelier

## Platform
web (100% client-side browser runtime, zero server telemetry)

## Users
Research analysts, biostatisticians, data journalists, educators, and students who need transparent, reproducible statistical workflows without uploading sensitive data to cloud servers.

## Product Purpose
Unite five specialized browser-native statistical laboratories into one continuous workflow:
1. **01 Prepare — DataMend Lab** (`https://rkohnmn.github.io/datamend-lab/` | `https://github.com/rkohnmn/datamend-lab`)
2. **02 Visualize — VisualGrammar** (`https://rkohnmn.github.io/visualgrammar/` | `https://github.com/rkohnmn/visualgrammar`)
3. **03 Investigate — QuartetLab** (`https://rkohnmn.github.io/quartetlab/` | `https://github.com/rkohnmn/quartetlab`)
4. **04 Infer — ResampleLab** (`https://rkohnmn.github.io/resamplelab/` | `https://github.com/rkohnmn/resamplelab`)
5. **05 Forecast — ForecastLab** (`https://rkohnmn.github.io/forecastlab/` | `https://github.com/rkohnmn/forecastlab`)

## Explicit Non-Goals
- No cloud database, login wall, or server-side CSV upload.
- No black-box p-values without graphical distribution diagnostics.
- No truncated bar charts, dual-axis distortion, or colorblind-hostile rainbow palettes.
