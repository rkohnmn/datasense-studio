# Editorial & Statistical Copywriting Guidelines

## 1. Voice & Tone
- Write in clear, front-loaded operational prose (grammatical subject and verb within the first 6–8 words).
- Avoid AI marketing clichés ("revolutionize", "unlock the power", "delve", "tapestry", "enterprise-grade", "trusted by thousands").
- Never fabricate customer logos, star counts, user counts, or institutional endorsements.

## 2. Statistical Accuracy Rules
- Distinguish random sampling (supports generalization to a population) from random assignment (supports causal comparison).
- Distinguish parameter confidence intervals from future-observation prediction intervals.
- State explicitly that bootstrap resampling quantifies sampling variability from an observed sample and cannot repair selection bias.
