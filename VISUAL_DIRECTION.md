# Visual Direction & Art Direction Specification

## 1. Aesthetic Lineage
The visual system synthesizes 1970s Bell Labs statistical monographs, John Tukey's *Exploratory Data Analysis*, and Hewlett-Packard benchtop instrument manuals into **The Modern Statistical Atelier**.

## 2. Compositional Rhythm
To prevent repetitive SaaS card walls, each homepage section uses a distinct structural grammar:
1. **Header**: Restrained sticky glass-paper bar with keyboard search trigger (`/`).
2. **Hero**: Asymmetric 5.2fr / 6.8fr split pairing oversized serif editorial copy with an interactive 5-stage live workbench preview switcher.
3. **Introduction to the Labs**: 12-column asymmetric Bento grid (7-col featured QuartetLab + 5-col DataMend Lab on Row 1; three 4-col panels on Row 2).
4. **Product Showcases**: Alternating left/right 5fr/7fr editorial spreads with a full dark-mode instrument contrast panel for QuartetLab.
5. **Interactive Analytical Journey**: 5-stage horizontal stepper collapsing cleanly to a vertical sequence on mobile.
6. **Find Your Tool**: Split interactive task selector and live recommendation card.
